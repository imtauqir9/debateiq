Cricket & Food · 5-Layer AI Reasoning · ElevenLabs Voice

1
Pick Cricket or Food — then click a topic
Click a category button to switch, then click a topic card to select your debate question.

Choose position, persona & tone
Select your side from the dropdown. Pick a persona (Analyst, Journalist, Lawyer…) and a debate tone to shape the AI's style.

Set a source, click Run — watch AI reason live
Pick ESPNCricinfo, WHO, BBC etc. or paste any URL. 5 reasoning layers fire one by one. Optionally connect ElevenLabs to hear it read aloud.
